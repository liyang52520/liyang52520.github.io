## 🎉 Welcome to My Blog!

Hello, world! This is my brand new blog powered by **Hexo** — a fast, simple, and powerful static blog framework.

### What to Expect

Here I'll be sharing thoughts on:

- **Technology** — coding, tools, and software engineering
- **Life** — experiences, travel, and hobbies
- **Learning** — tutorials, tips, and resources

### Why Hexo?

Hexo makes blogging effortless:

- ⚡ **Blazing fast** — powered by Node.js
- 📝 **Markdown support** — write in your favorite editor
- 🎨 **Beautiful themes** — customize to your heart's content
- 🚀 **One-click deploy** — push to GitHub Pages, Vercel, and more
